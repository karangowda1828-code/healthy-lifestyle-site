Healthy Lifestyle

A healthy lifestyle means taking care of your body and your mind. If we eat good food, exercise daily and sleep on time then we can stay away from many diseases.

  • Wake up early
  • Do not skip breakfast
  • Avoid too much mobile and TV
  • Think positive

Health Awareness

Many people do not know about their health problems. Some important points:

  • Wash your hands before eating
  • Go to doctor for regular checkup
  • Do not smoke or chew tobacco
  • Keep your surroundings clean
  • Take care of mental health, talk to someone if you feel sad
